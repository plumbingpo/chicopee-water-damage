Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Chicopee

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
